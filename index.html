The problem

A new asset class with
no track record.

EVs and solar systems haven't existed long enough to generate decades of default data. So lenders across the Global South stay on the sidelines — even where the climate impact per dollar deployed is highest. That hesitation is a $4T+ financing gap standing between proven clean technology and the people who need it.

Currently — Founding Engineer @ Hyfin

Hyfin turns clean energy
into a bankable asset.

Hyfin is a climate fintech company unlocking clean energy financing across South & Southeast Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa — through off-balance-sheet SPVs for EV loan receivables (CMRF), first/second-loss guarantee facilities, and blended finance instruments that de-risk lending into a market that's brand new.

What I'm building — GAIA

The confidence decades
of history used to provide.

I'm building GAIA, an AI platform that gives lenders the data infrastructure and risk tools to originate clean energy loans with confidence. GAIA synthesizes fragmented signals — asset performance, borrower behavior, market conditions — into underwriting intelligence, replacing decades of historical track record that simply doesn't exist yet.

Why it matters

Compressing the timeline
to bankability.

Every year a lender waits for "enough data" is a year of avoidable emissions. GAIA exists to shrink that timeline — right where the climate impact per financed dollar is highest, and accelerate how fast clean tech adoption can scale.
