Utila · 4 – 30 May 2026

Under water on Utila

Below the surface, the internship covered three kinds of work: growing and checking coral, counting and identifying what lives on the reef, and taking out an invasive predator.

Slate 01

Coral restoration

  • Coral nursery work
  • Monitoring out-planted coral fragments
  • Coral recruit monitoring
  • SCTLD treatment (stony coral tissue loss disease)

Slate 02

Surveys & identification

  • REEF fish surveys
  • ID of reef-associated organisms
  • Coral ID
  • Megafauna monitoring

Slate 03

Invasive species

  • Lionfish removal
  • Lionfish dissection

Lionfish are invasive in the Caribbean and eat native reef fish, so every one removed helps.
