Celine Willemsen: five site drafts

Same content, five directions. Open each one on a phone and a laptop. Grey dashed boxes are photo slots waiting for real pictures.

  • 01

    Field Journal

    A naturalist's expedition notebook: taped-in photos, a hand-drawn tree of life as her timeline, specimen sketches and a sepia chart of field stations.

    Image slots
  • 02

    Dive Log

    Scrolling is a descent from the sunlit surface to the seabed, with a live depth gauge, logbook entries per placement and PADI stamps.

    Image slots
  • 03

    Canopy to Coral

    One continuous transect: rainforest canopy, understory, river, mangroves, reef, deep reef and seabed, with illustrated scenery in every zone.

    Image slots
  • 04

    Expedition Map

    An interactive world map tracing her route. Tap a stop to open its field card; everything is also laid out below the map.

    Image slots
  • 05

    Magazine Feature

    A long-form cover story in a fictional field-ecology magazine, TRANSECT: big serif type, photo spreads and a field file for each chapter.

    Image slots
Real photos go in celine/photos/ (see the list of filenames); every draft picks them up automatically.
