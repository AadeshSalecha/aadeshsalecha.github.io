Now

Studying biodiversity in Göttingen

Hi, I'm Celine. In October 2026 I started a B.Sc. in Biological Diversity and Ecology at the University of Göttingen.

I came to biology from business. After years in finance at LEMKEN, I spent 2025 and 2026 doing hands-on conservation work: on coral reefs in South Africa and Honduras, in the rainforest of the Peruvian Amazon, and with my local BUND group at home. Along the way I trained up to PADI Rescue Diver.

Long term, I want to do ecological field work: conservation, biodiversity monitoring, and reef and rainforest ecology.
